The inventory includes runtime and build-only dependency license metadata.
Cargo.toml records upstream package authors and declared SPDX choices.
custom_debug and custom_debug_derive 0.6.2 declare Apache-2.0 OR MIT;
Apache-2.0.txt supplies the Apache-2.0 alternative. Their published crate
archives do not include a separate LICENSE/NOTICE file.
imgui-license.txt is the vendored IHS ImGui version's upstream license;
sources.json records its download and hash.
Argo and Veloce do not currently have a root license file. This inventory
records that gap; it does not grant a license to those projects or establish
that this development artifact is ready for public redistribution.
